INTRODUCTION TO RSI (Relative Strength Index)

RSI is a momentum indicator that tells you how strong or weak the price movement is.

It moves between 0 and 100.

✔ Above 70 → Overbought

Price has moved up too fast. Market may cool down or pull back.

✔ Below 30 → Oversold

Price has fallen too fast. Market may bounce or recover.

✔ Around 50 → Neutral

Buyers and sellers are equal. Trend decision zone.

In simple words:

RSI shows the energy and strength behind the price.

If RSI is rising → momentum is rising.

If RSI is falling → momentum is falling.

If price moves without momentum → something is wrong.

This is where divergence comes in.

⭐ INTRODUCTION TO DIVERGENCE

Divergence happens when Price and RSI don’t agree.

Normally, they move together:

Price makes higher highs → RSI also makes higher highs

Price makes lower lows → RSI makes lower lows

But when they disagree, it signals a big shift.

✔ Bullish Divergence

Price makes Lower Low

RSI makes Higher Low

Meaning: Price looks weak, but momentum is getting stronger. Reversal upward may come soon.

✔ Bearish Divergence

Price makes Higher High

RSI makes Lower High

Meaning: Price looks strong, but inside momentum is dying. Reversal downward may come soon.

✔ Hidden Divergence

This is not a reversal signal — it is a trend continuation signal.

Hidden Bullish → Uptrend continues

Hidden Bearish → Downtrend continues

It appears when momentum dips deeper than price, showing stored strength.

⭐ Why RSI + Divergence is Powerful

RSI tells you how strong the market truly is, while Divergence shows you when the strength is changing before price changes.

That’s why traders love it:

It warns before reversals

It confirms trend continuation

It helps avoid trap trades

It prevents chasing weak moves

Think of it as:

Price shows the surface. RSI shows the truth underneath.

When the surface and the truth don’t match → divergence → trading opportunity.

🔥 1️⃣ STRONG DIVERGENCE (Most Powerful)

🔹 Strong Bullish Divergence

Price: Makes a Lower Low (LL)

Indicator: Makes a Higher Low (HL)

Interpretation:

Strong sellers in price

Weak sellers in momentum

Buyers entering quietly → BIG reversal probability

This is the best reversal signal.

🔻 Strong Bearish Divergence

Price: Makes Higher High (HH)

Indicator: Makes Lower High (LH)

Interpretation:

Price is pumped by late buyers

Momentum is dying

Big correction likely

This is the strongest short signal.

🟡 2️⃣ MEDIUM DIVERGENCE (Good, but not perfect)

🔹 Medium Bullish Divergence

Price: Makes Equal Low (double bottom)

Indicator: Makes Higher Low

Interpretation:

Price holding support

Indicator showing hidden strength

Good bounce probability, slightly weaker than strong divergence.

🔻 Medium Bearish Divergence

Price: Makes Equal High (double top)

Indicator: Makes Lower High

Interpretation:

Price is testing resistance

Indicator is weakening

Cool-off expected, but maybe not a full reversal.

🟠 3️⃣ WEAK DIVERGENCE (Least reliable)

🔹 Weak Bullish Divergence

Price: Makes Lower Low (LL)

Indicator: Makes Equal Low

Interpretation:

Indicator is showing no new weakness

Not strong enough alone

Works better when near support/FVG/OB

🔻 Weak Bearish Divergence

Price: Makes Higher High (HH)

Indicator: Makes Equal High

Interpretation:

Slight loss of momentum

Weak signal → don’t use alone

Combine with resistance, supply zones, or VWAP rejection.

🫥 4️⃣ HIDDEN DIVERGENCE (Trend continuation signal)

This is opposite of normal divergence. Hidden divergence signals trend continuation, not reversal.

🔹 Hidden Bullish Divergence (Uptrend Continuation)

Price: Makes a Higher Low (HL)

Indicator: Makes a Lower Low (LL)

Interpretation:

Price protects the trend

Indicator shows excessive pessimism

Smart money accumulating → uptrend continuation

Most powerful continuation signal.

🔻 Hidden Bearish Divergence (Downtrend Continuation)

Price: Makes Lower High (LH)

Indicator: Makes Higher High (HH)

Interpretation:

Price rejects resistance

Indicator overbought but price not higher

Trend continues down

Strong continuation short signal.

✅ 1) DIVERGENCE FAILURE GUIDE

Why divergence fails, when it fails, and how to avoid false signals.

⚠️ WHY DIVERGENCE FAILS

Divergence does NOT mean reversal. It only means momentum is slowing. Price can still continue in the same direction because of:

1️⃣ Strong Trend Conditions

If market is trending hard, divergences will fail repeatedly.

Clue:

RSI stays above 60–70 = strong uptrend → bearish divergence FAILS

RSI stays below 30–40 = strong downtrend → bullish divergence FAILS

👉 Trend > Indicator Never counter strong trend using divergence.

2️⃣ Wrong Pivot Points

Divergence is valid only on CLEAN swing highs/lows. If you force a divergence between random candles → fail.

3️⃣ No Liquidity Sweep

True reversals usually start after liquidity grab. If divergence forms without liquidity being taken → weak setup.

Example: Price doesn’t break previous swing low, yet RSI shows HL → weak divergence.

4️⃣ Wrong Timeframe

Most failures occur when traders look only at small timeframe (1m, 3m) and ignore macro trend.

Rule:

Check higher timeframe momentum (5m > 15m > 1h). If higher TF disagrees → divergence fails.

5️⃣ Divergence After Expansion Candle

If a huge momentum candle forms before divergence → continuation is likely, not reversal.

6️⃣ No Structure Shift

Even with divergence, reversal needs:

Break of structure (BOS)

Retest

Follow-through

If structure doesn’t break → divergence becomes useless.

📌 DIVERGENCE FAILURE = BIG TREND CONTINUATION SETUP

The best times to trade with trend: → When you see 3–4 failed divergences in a row.

Meaning: Smart money is squeezing counter-trend traders. Trend is extremely strong.
