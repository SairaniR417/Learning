🧱 MODULE 1: MARKET STRUCTURE — The Language of Price

“Before you trade price, learn to read its rhythm.”

🔹 1.1 What Is Market Structure?

Market structure is how price organizes itself — a sequence of higher highs (HH), higher lows (HL), lower highs (LH), and lower lows (LL) that form the story of buyers and sellers.

It tells us:

Who is in control (buyers or sellers)

When control is shifting

Where the next move will likely go

Every price movement is structure, not noise.

🔹 1.2 Price Moves in Waves

Price moves in waves — not lines.

Each wave has two parts:

Impulse Move: A strong directional push caused by institutional order flow.

Corrective Move: A slower retracement that collects liquidity before the next impulse.

Together they form structure:

Impulse → Correction → Impulse → Correction

In every market, on every timeframe, this wave logic repeats endlessly.

🔹 1.6 Break of Structure (BOS)

Definition: A Break of Structure happens when price closes beyond the previous swing point in the direction of the trend.

Bullish BOS → breaks last high

Bearish BOS → breaks last low

Components:

A clear swing high/low

A strong impulse candle

Candle close confirmation

What it means: Institutions continue driving price in the same direction.

(Visual: HH → HL → BOS → HH)

🔹 1.7 Change of Character (CHOCH)

Definition: A CHOCH occurs when price breaks structure in the opposite direction — signaling a possible reversal.

Example: If price was HH → HL → HH → then breaks HL → CHOCH confirmed → possible downtrend begins.

Validation Rule: Price must close beyond the HL/LH level — not just wick it. A wick break = liquidity grab, not structure shift.

(Visual: Bullish structure fails → CHOCH → Bearish begins)

🔹 1.9 Textbook vs Reality

In textbooks, structure looks like clean zig-zags. In reality, markets are messy — structure shifts include:

Minor BOS/CHOCH inside one larger swing

Liquidity sweeps before true breaks

False continuations before real reversals

Learn to see intent, not just pattern.
