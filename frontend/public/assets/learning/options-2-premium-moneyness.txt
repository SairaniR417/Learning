OPTIONS DAY 2: Anatomy of Premium & Moneyness

Title: Deconstructing the Option Premium — Intrinsic, Extrinsic & the Power of Moneyness

🎯 Objective of Day 2

By the end of this session, learners should:

Understand the composition of every option’s premium.

Be able to separate Intrinsic Value (real worth) from Extrinsic Value (hope & time).

Grasp how Moneyness (ITM, ATM, OTM) affects price, Greeks, and probability.

Feel the non-linear decay of options over time.

💡 Core Idea

“Every option’s price is a blend of what exists now and what the market expects to happen before expiry.”

The first part is Intrinsic (fact). The second is Extrinsic (expectation).

🔍 1. Understanding Option Premium

Every option has a total price, called Premium. But that price is not arbitrary — it’s composed of two distinct parts.

[ \text{Option Premium} = \text{Intrinsic Value} + \text{Extrinsic Value} ]

⚙️ 2. Intrinsic Value (The Real Core)

It’s the real, tangible value if the option were exercised right now.

Call Option: Intrinsic = Max(Spot – Strike, 0)

Put Option: Intrinsic = Max(Strike – Spot, 0)

If the option is already profitable now, it’s “In the Money” — it has Intrinsic Value.

⏳ 3. Extrinsic Value (The Hope, Time, & Volatility Component)

It’s the portion of the premium that exists because of time left + expected movement.

[ \text{Extrinsic Value} = \text{Premium} - \text{Intrinsic Value} ]

It’s the hope premium — how much the market is willing to pay for the chance that the option will move in-the-money before expiry.

📈 5. Time Decay – The Invisible Force

Time Decay (Theta) eats away the Extrinsic Value as expiry nears. But it doesn’t do it linearly — it’s exponential.

📉 Time vs Decay Curve

🧠 Insight:

In the first few days, decay is mild.

Last 2–3 days → rapid crash.

Sellers thrive here (they collect Theta).

Buyers lose edge unless momentum saves them.

⚡ Volatility’s Impact (Vega)

If volatility (IV) rises → premiums inflate. If volatility falls → premiums shrink.

Even with no price change, IV movement can alter your P/L.

🧩 Real-Life Analogy

Think of extrinsic value as “movie hype.” Before release → high hype (IV). After release → truth revealed → hype collapses (IV crush). The “time to release” (expiry) determines how long hype sustains.

🧘 9. Key Takeaways of Day 2

Option Premium = Intrinsic (fact) + Extrinsic (hope).

Moneyness (ITM/ATM/OTM) defines behavior, Greeks, and risk.

Time decay kills extrinsic non-linearly.

Volatility expands or crushes premiums regardless of price.

A trader’s job is to align moneyness + volatility + time with market structure.

🧠 “Smart traders don’t just trade options — they trade time, volatility, and probability through moneyness.”
