⚙️ 4️⃣ Delta Flow – How Market Makers Control Balance

Market makers typically maintain Delta Neutral portfolios:

They sell options (collect Theta).

They hedge Delta by buying/selling futures.

As underlying moves:

Delta of sold options changes.

They adjust hedge → causing secondary momentum.

🧠 Result:

During fast trends → Delta hedging amplifies the move.

During ranges → Delta hedging absorbs volatility.

💬 Example:

If Market Makers sold massive 22,000 Calls (Δ=0.50) and NIFTY rises 100 pts → Their Delta exposure increases → they buy futures → push price up more (gamma squeeze).

This is how institutional Delta flow creates short-covering rallies or collapses.

⚙️ 6️⃣ Building Your “Option Flow Map” (Daily Framework)

🧭 Step-by-Step Process:

Check Market Structure:

BOS / CHoCH / Range → identify direction.

Observe IV Trend:

Rising or falling? Expansion or crush?

Analyze OI Flow:

Are new positions being added or closed?

Calculate Delta Bias:

More Calls ITM or more Puts ITM?

Infer Edge:

If Delta + IV rising → Buyer control.

If Theta + OI rising → Seller control.

Execute Aligned Strategy:

Buyer edge → Buy Calls/Puts or Debit Spread.

Seller edge → Credit Spread / Iron Condor / Strangle.

⚙️ 8️⃣ The 3-Point “Flow Confirmation Rule”

Before entering any option trade:

Structure confirmation → BOS or Range clarity.

Volatility direction → IV expansion or contraction.

Flow alignment → Delta & OI movement in your favor.

If all 3 align → high probability trade. If only 1 aligns → stay out.
