DELTA TO DHARMA

Trade Like a Monk, Profit Like a Pro

Master your mind before, during & after the trade.

1️ Morning Mindset Reset

Why it matters: The pre-market mental state is the launch pad for your day’s performance. If you start with clutter, revenge thoughts, or fear of missing out, you’ve already lost half the battle.

What to do:

3-min Mind Sweep: Write down anything non-market-related bothering you.

Breathing Reset: 4-7-8 breathing (inhale 4 sec, hold 7 sec, exhale 8 sec) → reduces cortisol and anxiety.

One-Liner Focus Statement: Example → “I trade my plan, not my emotions.”

Tips, Tricks & Hacks:

Keep a "trigger list" of emotional states (boredom, anger, excitement) → if you feel them, pause trading.

Use a music cue — one calming instrumental track you only play pre-market to anchor focus.

Hack: Touch your desk and say “reset” when you catch yourself thinking about yesterday’s losses.

2️ Execution Discipline

Why it matters: A solid strategy means nothing if you cut winners early, average down blindly, or enter late from FOMO.

What to do:

Pre-market → Define exact entry triggers, SL, and target zones.

No Ad-lib Rule: No changing plan mid-trade unless new information invalidates it.

Trade Ticket Ritual: Say out loud the reason before clicking buy/sell.

Tips, Tricks & Hacks:

Use visual stop-loss markers (horizontal lines) before entry — removes hesitation.

2-strike rule: After two bad trades, step away for 30 minutes.

Keep a micro checklist taped on your screen:

Is the setup valid?

SL placed?

Risk ≤ 1R?

Emotion check done?

3️ Emotional Control

Why it matters: You’re not fighting the market — you’re fighting your own chemical reactions.

What to do:

Recognize the Hope → Euphoria → Fear → Despair → Acceptance cycle.

Label emotions as they arise (“I feel greed” instead of “I must get this win”).

Keep water nearby → Hydration helps regulate brain function & impulse control.

Tips, Tricks & Hacks:

Hack: Stand up during trades — it shifts body state & reduces emotional lock-in.

Set a Profit Ceiling: Stop trading after hitting daily goals — avoid giving back.

Use a loss normalization mantra: “Losses are tuition, not punishment.”

4️ Mid-Day Resets

Why it matters: Energy and focus dip mid-session → sloppy execution follows.

What to do:

Stand, stretch, or walk for 2 minutes every 60–90 min.

Revisit your morning plan — has the market context changed?

Skip trading during your personal “low focus hours” (track this over weeks).

Tips, Tricks & Hacks:

Keep a “market mood board” → sticky notes with market bias & key levels in plain sight.

Hack: Change screen brightness slightly mid-day → tricks brain into “fresh start” mode.

5️ Post-Market Reflection

Why it matters: Reviewing while emotions are fresh locks in lessons faster.

What to do:

Rate your day 1–10 for: Setup quality, Execution, Emotional control.

Write 2 wins + 1 improvement point.

Screenshot 1 trade you’re proud of (builds positive memory bank).

Tips, Tricks & Hacks:

Don’t look at P&L first → Review decision quality first.

Record a 60-sec voice note about key learning → review on weekends.

Hack: Store “highlight trades” in a separate folder for confidence on bad days.

6️ Confidence & Flow State Triggers

Why it matters: Confidence reduces hesitation; flow state makes execution automatic.

What to do:

Have a pre-trade ritual (e.g., sip water, deep breath, re-read setup).

Trade your A+ setups first before touching anything else.

Keep distractions physically out of reach (phone in drawer).

Tips, Tricks & Hacks:

Use chunking — group trades into 2–3 sequences, review between sequences.

Flow trigger: Small challenge + known skill = engagement without stress.

Review your longest winning streak week before big trading days — mental priming works.

7 Post-Market Mind Recovery

Debrief Without Drama

Mark trades as: A – Executed well, B – Partial discipline, C – Plan abandoned.

Ask: What emotion drove each trade?

Daily Emotional Graph

Quick sketch: Energy vs. Confidence during the session. Look for patterns.

Reinforce Wins (Even Small Ones)

Did you avoid a bad trade? Count it as a win.

Note at least one thing to repeat tomorrow.

Reset Ritual

End-of-day mental close — walk, workout, journal.

No charts or market talk after reset time.

No chasing candles.

Never trade just to make back losses.

The market is a marathon, not a sprint.

Protect mental capital before financial capital.

Trading Emotions & How to Master Them

Fear – The paralyzing “What if I lose?” thought. Control Trick: Trade only with pre-defined risk per trade (e.g., 1–2% of capital). Use a stop-loss before entering, so fear doesn’t get a seat at the table.

Greed – “One more trade… double or nothing.” Control Trick: Set daily profit targets and walk away once hit. Remember: the market will be here tomorrow, but your capital might not.

FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) – Chasing a move after it’s already gone. Control Trick: Replace chasing with planning — if you missed it, write the setup in your journal for next time. “No plan, no trade.”

Euphoria – Feeling invincible after a win streak. Control Trick: Enforce a “cool-off” rule — after big wins, take a break. Review your trades instead of upping your size recklessly.

Frustration – Losing patience when trades don’t work out. Control Trick: Reduce position size and trade only A+ setups until your head clears.

Overconfidence – “I can’t be wrong.” Control Trick: Back-test every new idea and keep an accountability partner or trading buddy who can question your moves.

Acceptance – The healthiest emotion in trading. Control Trick: Accept losses as the “cost of doing business.” Shift focus from win/loss to following your process perfectly.

Golden Rule: Your goal is not to kill emotions — it’s to trade in a way that your emotions don’t control the next click of your mouse.
