Advanced Options Structures

1. Why Multi-Leg Structures Matter

Single options = fast P/L, but also fast damage. Multi-leg = controlled risk, defined behaviour, predictable Greeks. You basically shift from gambling → engineering.

2. Main Advanced Structures We Use

Keep it simple. These are the only ones you need to master:

a) Ratio Spreads

Example: Buy 1 ATM, Sell 2 OTM

Best for: Directional + expecting IV crush

Advantage: Cheap entry, high reward if price stalls near sold strike

Risk: Sharp move beyond the 2nd short leg → unlimited loss

When I use it: Trending markets, but close to exhaustion.

b) Diagonal Spreads

Front-month + Next-month combination

Buy long-dated option, sell short-dated option

Best for: Directional but slow move + theta income

Why it works: Front month decays faster → pays you to wait

Perfect for monthly trend trades.

c) Calendar Spreads

Same strike, different expiries

Best for: Low IV environments

Works when: IV is expected to rise + price stays near strike

Why it works: You buy low IV, sell high IV later

Used for mean-reversion or range-bound conditions.

d) Double Diagonals

A calendar + a diagonal combined

Best for: Range-bound view with IV expansion expected

Safest way to play uncertain news/event weeks.

3. How These Behave in Weekly vs Monthly Expiry

Weekly Expiries

Fast theta decay

Violent gamma movement

Short legs become risky very fast

Ratio spreads + calendars behave sharper

Good for small, quick directional bets

Monthly Expiries

Smooth theta

More stable Greek curves

Pricing more logical

Diagonals, double diagonals shine

Good for traders who want controlled P/L curve

4. How to Pick Strikes the Smart Way

Most traders randomly choose strikes. Professionals use Delta, IV, and structure behavior:

For Ratio Spreads

Buy: 0.40–0.50 delta

Sell: 0.20–0.25 delta (x2)

For Diagonals

Buy: 0.35–0.45 delta next-monthly

Sell: 0.20–0.25 delta weekly

For Calendars

Always pick ATM or closest to ATM

Because calendars want price to stay near strike

5. When to Use Which Strategy

Very simple rulebook:

Strong trend: Diagonal, Ratio

Range-bound: Calendar, Double Diagonal

IV Low: Calendar

IV High: Diagonal (only when expecting contraction)

Before events: Double Diagonal

After events: Ratio Spread (IV crash play)

7. Mistakes to Avoid

Using debit spreads in low IV

Using ratio spreads when VIX is already high

Selling too close to ATM in weekly expiry

Not planning exit when short leg goes ITM

Holding adjustments too late

Not tracking Greek drift (especially delta)

ADJUSTMENTS MASTERCLASS

Lock Profits • Reduce Risk • Convert Losing Trades • Manage Delta Drift

1. The 3 Reasons We Adjust

Every adjustment falls into one of these:

A) Price moved fast (Delta drift)

→ Your strikes are now wrong.

B) IV changed (Vega shock)

→ Your structure is overpriced/underpriced.

C) Time passed (Theta decay)

→ Your longs weakened / shorts strengthened.

If you know which is happening, you know how to adjust.

**2. First Rule: Never Adjust for “Ego”

Adjust only for “Risk–Reward Shift.”**

If your structure is already giving 1:3+ RR, then:

trail aggressively

reduce exposure

lock-in from short legs

Never adjust to “prove your view right.”

3. Universal Adjustment Tools

You only need these tools:

1️⃣ Roll Up / Roll Down

Shift strikes to bring Delta back to target.

2️⃣ Add Wings

Convert naked risk → defined risk.

3️⃣ Convert Single Leg → Spread

Reduce exposure / lock partial profits.

4️⃣ Convert Spread → Iron Structure

Double hedge both sides.

5️⃣ Book Shorts, Keep Longs

Instant risk reduction + keep potential reward.

6️⃣ Reduce Quantity

Most underrated but most powerful.

4. How to Lock Profits (Simple Rules)

✔ If you’re directional and price moved in your favour:

→ Roll the short leg closer. This increases credit and locks profit.

Example: Bought 18400 CE, sold 18600 CE Price rallies → shift short 18600 → 18700 → lock.

✔ If the structure is already 60–70% max profit:

→ Cut the position to half Keep only runners.

✔ If IV crashed and you’re in debit spreads:

→ Close spread, leave runner long You already got the IV crush.

✔ When your long option doubles:

→ Sell 30–50% of quantity Reduce stress → lock reward.

5. How to Reduce Risk (Gold Rules)

✔ Add Wings (Convert to Iron)

If your short strike is too close or markets volatile:

Debit spread → Iron Fly

Credit spread → Iron Condor

Risk becomes fixed.

✔ Roll out to next expiry

If your view is right but time is killing:

Roll long option to next expiry

Roll short strike with it

✔ Move to lower delta

Shift to safer zones:

From 0.50 delta to 0.35 delta long

Short leg from 0.25 delta to 0.15 delta

✔ Convert naked long → Vertical

Lowers theta burn.

6. Adjustments For Each Strategy

● A) Debit Spreads (Bull Call / Bear Put)

When profit comes:

✔ Sell short leg & convert into naked long for runner ✔ Or roll the short leg closer to reduce cost ✔ Trail the long leg with stop-loss on premium

When going wrong:

✔ Roll down the long leg (for calls) to reduce cost ✔ Convert into butterfly to reduce loss ✔ Exit early — debit spreads go to zero fast

● B) Credit Spreads (Bear Call / Bull Put)

When profit comes:

✔ Close 50–70% early (credit spreads return fast) ✔ Roll untested side to collect more premium ✔ Convert into Iron Condor (double income)

When going wrong:

✔ Roll tested side further OTM ✔ Add debit hedge on tested side ✔ Don’t average — hedge instead

● C) Ratio Spreads

When profit comes:

✔ Take profit on extra short legs ✔ Add cheap wings (OTM long) to cap unlimited risk ✔ Roll the longs up (for CE ratios) or down (PE ratios)

When going wrong:

✔ Add wing to cap risk ✔ Close 1 short leg to stop unlimited exposure ✔ Roll entire structure to next expiry

● D) Calendar & Diagonals

When profit comes:

✔ Roll the short leg closer to ATM ✔ Increase credit → lower net cost ✔ Keep rolling until long leg has full value

When going wrong:

✔ Roll the short further OTM for breathing space ✔ Shift strike of long leg to reduce delta ✔ Do NOT hold when IV collapses

● E) Iron Condor / Iron Fly

When profit comes:

✔ Book the untested side ✔ Narrow the wings of untested side → lock profits ✔ Keep rolling short legs inward

When going wrong:

✔ Roll the losing side away ✔ Convert to broken-wing condor ✔ Add debit hedge

7. Easiest Visual Rule (Follow This)

If IV moves → Adjust Wings (add hedges)

If Time moves → Adjust Short Legs (roll or close)

This is the cleanest, simplest universal framework.

The Adjustment Engine

How to protect capital, lock profits, and control risk — every time.

**1. Start With This:

“What changed?”**

Before adjusting, ask only one question:

Did Price Change? → Delta problem

This tells you the correct adjustment instantly.

2. The “Delta Drift” Adjustment Rules (Price Movement)

A) Price moves in your favour

✔ Roll shorts closer → lock profits ✔ Trail longs with premium stop ✔ Cut quantity by 30–50% at 60–70% profit ✔ Move shorts to keep Delta near your target

Example: 18400 CE (long) + 18600 CE (short) Price moved up → move short to 18700 → lock gains.

B) Price moves against you

✔ Roll tested side away ✔ Add debit hedge (cheap long) ✔ Shift structure to lower delta ✔ Exit quickly if debit spread (they die fast)

Example: Bull Put spread hit → roll 18500 PE to 18450 PE to create space.

3. The “Vega Shock” Adjustments (IV Changes)

A) IV crashes (after event)

✔ Book debit spreads ✔ Keep runners only ✔ Don’t re-enter immediately—premiums will be cheap ✔ Convert calendar → long option runner

B) IV rises (before event)

✔ Add wings to protect ✔ Avoid ratio spreads ✔ Roll short legs further OTM ✔ Turn spreads into iron condor/iron fly for defined risk

4. The “Theta Drain” Adjustments (Time Damage)

A) For Long Options

✔ Roll out (next expiry) ✔ Convert naked long → debit spread to reduce decay ✔ Book early if theta killing your view

B) For Short Options

✔ Roll short leg closer as time decays ✔ Close 50–70% early ✔ Convert credit spread → iron condor and take extra premium

5. Structure-wise Adjustment Playbook

A) Debit Spreads

If in Profit:

✔ Remove short leg → run with long ✔ Roll short leg closer → reduce cost ✔ Convert to Butterfly → lock profit

If in Loss:

✔ Close quickly (don’t fight theta) ✔ Roll long to lower strike ✔ Cut half quantity to reduce decay

B) Credit Spreads

If in Profit:

✔ Book 50–70% early ✔ Roll untested side closer ✔ Convert into Iron Condor for extra premium

If in Loss:

✔ Roll tested strike away ✔ Add opposite-direction debit hedge ✔ Don’t average — hedge only

C) Ratio Spreads

If in Profit:

✔ Square off extra shorts → lock money ✔ Add far OTM long to remove unlimited risk ✔ Roll long strike upward/downward to follow trend

If in Loss:

✔ Add wing immediately ✔ Close one short ✔ Shift entire structure to next expiry

D) Calendar / Diagonal

If in Profit:

✔ Roll short to ATM ✔ Increase credit every roll ✔ Keep the long until it has full value

If in Loss:

✔ Roll short further OTM for space ✔ Shift long strike to reduce delta ✔ Close quickly if IV collapse

E) Iron Fly / Iron Condor

If in Profit:

✔ Book untested side ✔ Tighten wings → lock profit ✔ Roll winning side inward

If in Loss:

✔ Roll tested side away ✔ Add debit hedge (same side) ✔ Convert to broken-wing condor
