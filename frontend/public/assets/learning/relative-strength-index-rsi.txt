The RSI

(Relative Strength Index)

Chapter 1: The Foundations of RSI

1.1 What is RSI?

RSI = Relative Strength Index, developed by Welles Wilder (1978).

Purpose: Measure momentum strength — how fast and how far price moves.

Scale: 0 to 100.

Key Zones:

70 → Overbought.

<30 → Oversold.

40–60 → Neutral, consolidation.

⚠️ RSI doesn’t say “buy” or “sell” automatically — it shows energy of the crowd.

Chapter 2: The RSI Formula

RS = Average Gain ÷ Average Loss.

RSI = 100 – (100 ÷ (1 + RS)).

Default period = 14 (can adjust).

Wilder’s smoothing makes it responsive yet stable.

Chapter 3: RSI Core Interpretations

RSI > 70 = Buying momentum overheated.

RSI < 30 = Selling momentum overheated.

50 Midline:

Above 50 → Buyers control.

Below 50 → Sellers control.

Trend Ranges:

Bull trend: RSI holds between 40–80.

Bear trend: RSI holds between 20–60.

Chapter 4: RSI Divergences

4.1 Regular Divergence (Reversals)

Bullish: Price ↓, RSI ↑ → Weakness in bears.

Bearish: Price ↑, RSI ↓ → Weakness in bulls.

4.2 Hidden Divergence (Continuations)

Bullish: Price higher low, RSI lower low → Trend continuation.

Bearish: Price lower high, RSI higher high → Trend continuation.

⚡ Hidden divergences are pro tools — often missed by retail, used by institutions to trap.

Chapter 5: RSI Variants

Stochastic RSI → RSI of RSI, faster.

Connors RSI (CRSI) → Combines RSI(3) + Up/Down streaks + ROC(100). Great for short-term mean reversion.

RSI Bands → Apply Bollinger-style bands to RSI for breakout detection.

Laguerre RSI → Smooth, almost lag-free.

Chapter 6: RSI Regime Shifts

Bullish Regime: RSI shifts from 20–60 → 40–80.

Bearish Regime: RSI shifts from 40–80 → 20–60.

These range shifts = confirmation of major trend change.

📌 Example: NIFTY breaking ATH → RSI never drops below 40 = bull regime confirmed.

Chapter 7: RSI Trading Strategies

7.1 Swing Overbought/Oversold

Buy RSI < 30 turning up.

Sell RSI > 70 turning down. ⚠️ Use only in sideways markets.

7.2 Divergence Trading

Combine RSI divergence with support/resistance zones for high-probability reversals.

7.3 RSI + Trend Filter

Trade only long when RSI > 50.

Trade only short when RSI < 50.

7.4 RSI + VWAP Intraday

RSI(5–7) + VWAP → scalp reversals around VWAP.

7.5 RSI + ATR/ADX

ADX confirms strength.

ATR confirms volatility.

RSI pinpoints momentum entry. 👉 Together = lethal combo for trend trades.

Chapter 8: RSI Psychology

RSI > 80 = Euphoria & greed → smart money exits.

RSI < 20 = Fear & panic → smart money accumulates.

RSI Divergences = Sentiment exhaustion before price reacts.

RSI 40–60 → Confusion phase, retail chopped up.

Chapter 9: RSI Risk Management

Stop-loss using RSI: Below swing low in bullish divergence.

Position sizing: Reduce size when ATR high but RSI signals reversal (avoid slippage).

Scaling in/out: Add at RSI 40 in uptrend, book profits near 70–80.

Chapter 10: RSI Playbooks

10.1 Intraday Scalping Playbook

RSI(5–7) oversold at demand → buy scalp.

RSI(5–7) overbought at supply → short scalp.

10.2 Swing Trading Playbook

RSI(14) divergence + trendline break.

Works best on daily chart.

10.3 Position Trading Playbook

Weekly RSI holding >40 = bullish continuation.

Use with moving averages for long-term trend following.

Chapter 11: Common Mistakes

Selling just because RSI > 70 (strong trends can stay above 70).

Buying just because RSI < 30 (bear trends keep RSI oversold).

Using same settings everywhere (tweak period: 9 for intraday, 14 for swing, 20–30 for position).

Ignoring context (always combine RSI with structure).

RSI

1. RSI as a Market X-Ray

Most retail traders misuse RSI as just an “overbought/oversold” indicator. Pro view: RSI = rate of acceleration & exhaustion in price.

👉 Think of RSI as a speedometer, not a “buy/sell” button.

Price can go fast (RSI stays >70) → trend continuation.

Price slows down (RSI divergence) → trend exhaustion.

RSI flatlines near 50 → no dominance, equilibrium zone.

2. RSI Core Structures

🔹 2.1 Levels & Ranges

Range-bound: 30–70

Bull market: 40–80 shift

Bear market: 20–60 shift

Pro tip: Don’t watch 70/30 blindly — see where RSI bases (40/50/60) to identify market regime.

🔹 2.2 RSI Zones & Bias

Above 60 = strong bullish structure

Below 40 = strong bearish structure

Between 40–60 = sideways chop

👉 Institutional traders read RSI zones like “market climate reports.”

3. Divergences — The Weapon

🔹 3.1 Regular Divergence (Reversal)

Price ↑, RSI ↓ = Bearish divergence

Price ↓, RSI ↑ = Bullish divergence

🔹 3.2 Hidden Divergence (Continuation)

Price ↑ with higher low, RSI ↓ with lower low → Bullish continuation

Price ↓ with lower high, RSI ↑ with higher high → Bearish continuation

🔹 3.3 Double Divergence

RSI diverges twice while price still trends.

Pro tip: Double divergence near HTF S/R = high probability sniper entry.

4. RSI Patterns (Most Traders Miss These)

👉 RSI can form chart patterns just like price:

RSI Trendline Breakout: often precedes price breakout.

RSI Head & Shoulders: reliable reversal clue.

RSI Double Bottom/Top: momentum shift early signal.

Institutional trick: Watch RSI trendline breaks before price breaks. RSI usually “leads.”

5. RSI Swing Rejection (Wilder’s Secret)

Wilder himself suggested this method (rarely taught now). Bullish Swing Rejection:

RSI falls below 30

Comes back above 30

Pulls back but stays above 30

Then turns up again → Buy signal

Bearish Swing Rejection: Reverse at 70.

⚡ Pro insight: Stronger when it aligns with demand/supply zones.

6. RSI Multi-Timeframe Mastery

Daily RSI trend + 15m RSI entry = sniper model

Example:

Daily RSI above 60 → bias bullish

Intraday RSI dips below 40 → entry opportunity in line with trend

Pro traders never trade RSI in isolation. They sync multiple TFs.

7. Advanced RSI Strategies

🔹 7.1 RSI + EMA Cloud

Add EMA (9 or 21) on RSI itself

RSI crossing above EMA = early long signal

RSI crossing below EMA = early short

🔹 7.2 RSI + Bollinger Bands

RSI breaches its own Bollinger Band → exhaustion extreme

Great for mean-reversion setups

🔹 7.3 RSI + Volume Profile

RSI divergence + price at HVN/POC = trend reversal hotspot

RSI divergence + price at LVN = fake breakout trap

🔹 7.4 RSI + Smart Money Concepts

Liquidity sweep + RSI divergence = sniper entry

Order block aligned with RSI swing rejection = high probability trade

8. RSI Scalping Hacks

RSI (2): For extreme scalps → buy <10, sell >90.

RSI (5) + RSI (14):

RSI(5) entry, RSI(14) trend filter.

Example: If RSI(14) > 50 (bull trend), only buy dips when RSI(5) < 20.

9. Pro Tips & Institutional Tricks

✅ Use RSI range shifts to filter false signals. ✅ Always combine RSI with price structure + liquidity zones. ✅ When RSI stays above 70/ below 30 for long → trend, not reversal. ✅ Draw support/resistance directly on RSI (not price) → reveals hidden momentum shifts. ✅ RSI “failure swings” = most powerful reversal clue. ✅ Use RSI as a confluence tool, not standalone.

10. Psychology Behind RSI

Retail: Think RSI = “buy low/sell high.”

Pro: Use RSI to detect momentum imbalance & institutional exhaustion.

Elite: Treat RSI as a context filter, not a signal.

👉 RSI = context. Price Action = entry. Risk Management = survival.
