Put–Call Ratio (PCR)

Understanding Market Bias Through Option Writers’ Positioning

🧩 1️⃣ What is PCR?

PCR (Put–Call Ratio) =Put Open Interest\Call Open Interest

It tells you whether more puts are written or more calls are written.

Why is this important?

Because option writers are smart money. They take risk only when they are confident.

Put writers are bullish.

Call writers are bearish.

So PCR reveals whether smart money is leaning bullish or bearish.

🧩 2️⃣ What PCR Actually Measures

PCR measures the confidence of writers, not buyers.

✔️ More Put writing → PCR ↑ → Writers expect market not to fall

✔️ More Call writing → PCR ↓ → Writers expect market not to rise

🧩 4️⃣ PCR + IV + OI = True Bias

PCR alone ≠ complete truth. PCR combined with IV + OI = institutional map.

🔹 Bullish Setup (Strong)

PCR rising

IV stable or rising

OI shifting to Puts

Delta drifting upward

🔹 Bearish Setup (Strong)

PCR falling

IV rising

OI shifting to Calls

Delta drifting downward

🔹 Range Setup

PCR around 1

IV falling

OI building both sides (strangles written)

Delta stable

🧩 5️⃣ PCR For Intraday Traders

Intraday = watch PCR change, not absolute number.

✔️ PCR Rising Intraday → Put writing → Bullish short-term

✔️ PCR Falling Intraday → Call writing → Bearish short-term

Use with structure:

PCR rising + BOS → strong intraday uptrend

PCR falling + CHoCH down → strong intraday downtrend

Avoid buying when:

PCR extremely high (>1.4) → reversal danger

IV dropping → premiums die

Avoid selling when:

PCR extremely low (<0.6) → reversal possible

IV rising → volatility explosion possible

🧩 6️⃣ PCR for Swing Traders

Swing traders rely on PCR of monthly expiries.

PCR 0.9–1.2 → safe zone for positional trades

PCR > 1.3 → market oversold; prepare for rally

PCR < 0.7 → market overbought; prepare for dip

PCR trend is more important than value:

Rising PCR → accumulation phase

Falling PCR → distribution phase

🧩 7️⃣ PCR for Expiry & Weekly Options

Weekly PCR is extremely useful because:

Gamma is high

Writers aggressively defend levels

Max Pain interacts with PCR

Reversal traps happen when PCR extremes occur

Expiry Day Logic:

PCR near 1 → market likely to stay in range

PCR < 0.7 → expiry may drift down

PCR > 1.2 → expiry may drift up (short covering rally)

🧩 8️⃣ Automatic PCR vs Manual PCR

✔️ Automatic PCR

Uses OI from ALL strikes

Good for overall sentiment

Works best on NIFTY/BankNIFTY

✔️ Manual PCR

Select ATM ± 3 strikes

Removes far OTM noise

Very powerful for intraday trend

More accurate than Automatic PCR

🧩 9️⃣ PCR Traps & How to Avoid Them

⚠️ PCR High (1.3–1.5) Trap

If IV rising → Put writers in trouble → big move can come.

⚠️ PCR Low (<0.6) Trap

If IV rising → Call writers in trouble → upside spike possible.

🧠 So rule is:

PCR extreme + IV rising = strong move coming

PCR extreme + IV falling = range compression

🧩 🔟 PCR + Max Pain = Expiry Prediction

PCR high → Puts strong → expiry tends to drift upward

PCR low → Calls strong → expiry tends to drift downward

PCR ≈ 1 → expiry tends to pin near Max Pain

Max Pain + PCR trend is one of the best expiry tools.
