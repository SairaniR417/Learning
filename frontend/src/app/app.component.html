BOOK 02 · CHART TOOLS · CHAPTER 01

Fibonacci Retracement

Price levels

Retracement levels divide a selected price swing using ratios associated with the Fibonacci sequence. Traders watch these as possible support or resistance areas during a pullback; price can pass through them without reacting.

How this dashboard draws it: it finds the lowest low and highest high in the loaded candles. If the low came first, it treats the range as an upswing and measures back down from the high. If the high came first, it treats it as a downswing and measures up from the low. This automatic full-range anchoring is convenient, but may not match the swing a trader would choose manually.

{{ level.percent }}{{ level.description }}

Example: for a rise from 100 to 200, the 61.8% retracement is 138.20 (200 − 100 × 0.618). The 50% level is widely used but is not a Fibonacci sequence ratio. Levels are reference areas, not guaranteed turning points or trade instructions.

These are educational descriptions of rule-based patterns and chart tools. They do not account for news, liquidity, or broader market conditions.
