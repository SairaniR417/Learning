One stock appears once. Ghost Rider keeps its latest crossover direction until an opposite crossover, as in Scanner; an old or stop-touched setup does not qualify as a fresh entry. Closed Suryavanshi trades and invalidated divergences are excluded. Opposing active signals are labelled CONFLICT and have no entry levels. Entry checks passed comes first, followed by stocks with more supporting strategies and newer signals. This order is not a profit forecast.

Before the next session, the shortlist is a watchlist based on the candle date shown. Stored scans do not fetch new broker candles. Refresh after syncing history; a new scan alone cannot make old prices current.

Daily signals use completed candles. Daily setups remain WATCH when the entry policy needs current-session candles or intraday daily-open levels.
